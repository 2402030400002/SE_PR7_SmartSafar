Kashmir's Rich Heritage

Kashmir is blessed with fertile land, a cool climate and abundant water resources, making horticulture an important part of the region's agricultural economy. Many families depend on fruit cultivation and related activities for their livelihood.

The valley is well known for apples, cherries, pears, walnuts, almonds and saffron. These products are valued for their quality and are supplied to markets across India and beyond.

Major Horticulture Products

Apple Orchards

Kashmir is one of India's major apple-producing regions. Apple cultivation is an important source of income for many orchard growers.

Saffron Fields

Pampore and surrounding areas are well known for the cultivation of high-quality Kashmiri saffron.
